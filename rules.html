Rules & game

How the competition actually works

Two stages, one identical challenge for everyone, and a live final to decide the winners.

The two stages

1

City challenge

Every team completes the exact same mission in their own city. No two teams get an easier or harder version — the task, the scoring, and the deadline are identical nationwide.

2

The final — Piatra Neamț

Teams that pass the city stage advance to a live final held in Piatra Neamț, where the top teams compete head-to-head to decide the season's winners.

📋 General rules

📄 Download full rules PDF
  • G1Same task, every city. Fairness comes first — nobody gets a locational advantage.
  • G2Team size and eligibility. Open to student teams, any experience level, with a coach or mentor.
  • G3Club structure. A club can register multiple teams, each in a different category (Robotics, Programming, Design, etc.).
  • G4Conduct. Standard fair-play and safety rules apply at every stage. Unsportsmanlike behavior results in disqualification.
  • E2Intellectual property. All work submitted must be the team's own. Plagiarism or unauthorized use of external code/designs leads to elimination.
  • A21Appeals. Each team can file up to 3 appeals per event day, referencing a specific timestamp in the live feed. After 3 rejected appeals, no further appeals are accepted.

🏆 Season challenge rules

  • S1City challenge (Stage 1). Every team completes the identical mission in their own city within the same deadline. Scoring is standardized across all cities.
  • S2Scoring rubric. Four criteria: Innovation (1-10), Technical Execution (1-10), Presentation (1-10), Teamwork (1-10). Maximum total: 40 points per judge.
  • S3Advancement. The highest-scoring teams from each city advance to the final. The exact cutoff depends on the number of participants.
  • S4The final (Stage 2). Held live in Piatra Neamț. Top teams compete head-to-head in a live-judged event. Results are final, subject only to the appeals process.
  • S5Prizes. Cash prizes for top finishers, sponsor vouchers for runners-up, and founding-team recognition for every team that participated in Season 1.
  • S6Certificates. Generated automatically once results are finalized and available for download from each club's profile.
