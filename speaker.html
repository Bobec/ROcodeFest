Speak at ROcodeFest

Bring your team a real problem to solve

Speakers are professionals — engineers, factory reps, industry experts — who present teams with a real-world challenge from their company for teams to solve or improve on.

What a ROcodeFest speaker does: instead of a typical presentation, you bring an actual problem your company faces — something teams can respond to with an idea, a design, or a working solution. It's a chance to get fresh perspective on a real challenge while inspiring the next generation of engineers.
