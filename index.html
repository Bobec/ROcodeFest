Who's behind this

Meet the organizers.

R

We started ROcodeFest because every great team needs a first shot.

We're a small crew of engineers and educators who wanted a robotics event where beginners feel as welcome as veterans. This is season one — and we're building it with you, not just for you.

Still on the fence?

Questions teams actually ask.

We've never competed before — can we still join?+

Yes. Most first-year teams have zero prior experience. That's exactly who this event is built for.

What if our robot isn't finished in time?+

Partial builds are welcome. Showing up and learning matters more than a perfect robot.

Is this a real, ongoing competition?+

Yes — this is our first season, and we're committed to running it every year going forward.

What does it cost to join?+

Registration is low-cost, and support is available for teams that need it.

Ready to be part of season one?

No robot, no experience, no problem. Just bring a team.
